Retrospend is a personal finance app for your own server. It tracks expenses, runs your budget, splits costs with other people and keeps your net worth, in any currency. This app is the Android client: connect it to your own Retrospend server, or to the free hosted one at retrospend.app.

<b>Money</b>
<ul>
<li>Expenses and income with categories and filters</li>
<li>Envelope budgets</li>
<li>Recurring expenses and income</li>
<li>Wealth: accounts and net worth</li>
<li>Every amount keeps its original currency</li>
<li>Added expenses can be undone</li>
</ul>

<b>Shared costs</b>
<ul>
<li>Split costs with other people</li>
<li>Projects for trips, households and group purchases</li>
<li>Settle up by recording a payment</li>
</ul>

<b>Yours</b>
<ul>
<li>Works without Google services. Notifications are checked on the phone.</li>
<li>Your data stays on your server, and you can export it</li>
<li>Free and open source under the GPL-3.0</li>
</ul>

To use the app you need a Retrospend server. Steps for running your own are at github.com/syntheit/retrospend.
