The wedge nobody else has

When you split an expense, only your share hits your budget. When a friend owes you, the receivable is visible next to your envelopes. When you settle up, everything reconciles itself.

People today run YNAB and Splitwise side-by-side and manually reconcile between them. Actual Budget has no splitting. Splitwise has no budgeting. SplitPro and Sure each own half the picture. Budget ⇄ split integration is Retrospend's flagship differentiator — every release below feeds it.

Envelope Budgeting

v0.6.0Planned

The flagship release. YNAB's philosophy — assign money you actually have, every dollar gets a job — rebuilt natively around Retrospend's splitting and multi-currency, and without YNAB's traps. It cannot be messy: simple by default, powerful on demand.

Income → “Ready to Assign”Flagship

Record income, then decide its jobs right there: what's saved, what's spent on what. Assign only money that exists.

Envelopes with real rolloverFlagship

Unspent balances carry forward automatically. Overspending pulls from next month's Ready to Assign — visible, never shameful.

Split-aware budgetsFlagship

Only your share of a shared expense hits your envelope. Receivables and settlements reconcile automatically. The YNAB+Splitwise killer.

Targets & sinking fundsFeature

Monthly targets, save-by-date goals, true-expenses smoothing for irregular bills.

Move money freelyFeature

Roll with the punches: cover overspending from another envelope in two clicks.

Graceful migrationInfra

Current limit-style budgets convert cleanly; the playground survives as what-if simulation on top of envelopes.

The Public API

v0.8.0Planned

Retrospend becomes a platform. REST + OpenAPI over the existing service layer — the door for the GNOME client, community integrations, and scripts.

REST /api/v1Infra

Hand-written route handlers over the service layer (not tRPC annotations). Start with ~20 endpoints: expenses, budgets, people, projects.

API keysInfra

Personal access tokens via better-auth's apiKey plugin: scoped, rate-limited, expiring. A Developer section in Settings.

OpenAPI spec + docsInfra

Generated from the existing Zod schemas. Typed client SDKs fall out for free — the Immich move.

OIDC / SSO loginFeature

The most-requested self-hoster auth feature. better-auth supports generic OIDC today.

The Immich Playbook

operating principles borrowed from the best self-hosted project of the decade
01Mobile is the product

Immich won because the app genuinely replaced Google Photos day-to-day. Retrospend's equivalent: expense entry so fast you stop using your notes app.

02API-first, SDK for free

OpenAPI spec generates typed clients for web, mobile, CLI, and community tools. One contract, many surfaces.

03Visible cadence

Frequent small releases with real notes signal the project is alive. Keep the /releases page honest and current.

04Ten-minute deploy

One docker-compose, one .env, published hardware minimums, and the top three misconfigurations documented up front.

05Demo everything

A live demo instance converts skeptics and satisfies app-store reviewers. Sample-data mode ships with the mobile app.

06Mission-first messaging

Every announcement leads with privacy and ownership, not feature lists. Year-in-review posts with real numbers build trust.

07Don't repeat YNAB's sins

No credit-card-handling homework, no overspending shame, no pricing rug-pulls — the three things that make YNAB users quit.

08Free means free

Splitwise's 3-expenses-a-day paywall created this market. The hosted tier can charge for convenience, never for features.
